01 / PRODUCTIVITY CONCEPT 2026

Bloom Study

A gentle academic planner concept designed to make student life feel more organised and less overwhelming.

The challenge

Students often balance deadlines, revision, personal commitments and rest across multiple tools. Bloom Study imagines a single calm space for seeing the week clearly.

Design approach

  • Soft visual hierarchy to make important tasks easy to scan.
  • Weekly overview with clear subject and priority cues.
  • Encouraging microcopy that makes planning feel achievable.

02 / BRAND IDENTITY CONCEPT 2026

Aster Studio

A visual identity concept for a small creative studio built around thoughtful ideas and expressive design.

The challenge

Create a memorable identity that feels warm, contemporary and confident without becoming overly formal.

Design approach

  • Burgundy and blush palette for warmth and distinction.
  • Expressive serif typography balanced with clean supporting text.
  • A simple star-flower mark that suggests curiosity and possibility.

03 / WEB EXPERIENCE CONCEPT 2026

Campus Connect

A responsive campus community concept to help students discover study groups, events and shared interests.

The challenge

It can be difficult to find classmates with similar interests or discover useful academic and social opportunities.

Design approach

  • Clear navigation for groups, events and student resources.
  • Card-based layout that works across desktop and mobile.
  • Friendly calls to action that encourage participation.
